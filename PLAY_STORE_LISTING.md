## Full Description (4000 chars max)
PackRight is your personal AI travel packing assistant. Simply describe your trip in natural language, and our intelligent packing advisor instantly generates a personalized checklist tailored to your destination, activities, accommodation, and travel style.

✨ KEY FEATURES:
• AI-Powered Lists: Describe your trip and get a smart packing list in seconds
• Destination-Aware: Suggests destination-specific items, plug adapters, cultural attire
• Activity-Smart: Knows what to pack for hiking, beaches, business, temples, and more
• Zero Friction: No forms, no sign-up, no gender selection — just describe and pack
• Offline Ready: Works completely offline once your list is generated
• Dark Mode: Beautiful dark theme for night packing
• Multi-Language: Generate lists in 9 languages
• Share & Collaborate: Share your packing list via WhatsApp, email, SMS, and more
• AI Follow-Up: Ask AI to add items as your plans change
• 100% Local: All your data stays on your device — no cloud sync, no accounts

🎯 HOW IT WORKS:
1. Tap "New Trip" and describe where you're going
2. Tell us about your activities, accommodation, and travel style
3. AI generates a comprehensive, personalized packing list
4. Check items off as you pack
5. Share with travel companions or keep it to yourself

🌍 SMART ABOUT DESTINATIONS:
• Beach trips: Snorkel gear, reef-safe sunscreen, waterproof pouch
• Mountain treks: Hiking boots, blister kit, altitude sickness remedy
• Business travel: Professional attire, tech accessories, portable charger
• Temple visits: Modest clothing, easy-to-remove footwear, head coverings
• Budget hostels: Padlock, towel, earplugs, flip-flops
• Luxury resorts: Formal wear, adapters, currency converter
• Multi-country tours: Multiple climate gear, universal chargers, visa copies

💡 NO DATA MINING:
PackRight respects your privacy. No accounts, no tracking, no analytics. Your trip descriptions are processed by Google's AI to generate lists, but nothing is stored on our servers. Delete the app and all your data is gone.

🚀 PERFECT FOR:
Frequent travelers, adventure seekers, family vacationers, business professionals, backpackers, cruise passengers, road trippers, and anyone who hates arriving at their destination only to realize they forgot something important.

Download PackRight today and pack smarter, not harder. 🧳
