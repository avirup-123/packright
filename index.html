Frequently Asked Questions about Travel Packing CheckList

Is PackRight free to use? expand_more
Yes, completely free. Just describe your trip and get your list.
How does the AI know what to suggest? expand_more
It analyzes your destination, travel dates, weather, planned activities, accommodation type, and travel style to build a list from scratch. Every list is unique to your trip.
Can I edit the packing list? expand_more
Yes. You can add your own items, remove anything you don't need, and check items off as you pack them.
Does it work for multi-city or multi-country trips? expand_more
Yes. Just mention all your stops. For example: '10 days - 3 in London, 4 in Paris, 3 in Barcelona.' The list covers all destinations.
What if the AI missed something? expand_more
You can manually add any item to any category. You can also ask the AI to add more by describing what changed - like 'also packing for a toddler' or 'add formal dinner clothes.'
