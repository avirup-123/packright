# Privacy Policy — PackRight

**Last Updated:** April 28, 2026

PackRight respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## Data We DON'T Collect

- **We do NOT collect personal information** — no name, email, or any identifiable data
- **We do NOT have user accounts or registration** — no sign-up required
- **We do NOT use analytics or tracking** — no Firebase Analytics, no crash reporting, no usage tracking
- **We do NOT sell or share data** — your data is yours alone

## Data Stored Locally

- **All your trips and packing lists are stored on your device only** using Hive local database
- **No data is synced to our servers** — everything stays on your phone
- **Deleting the app deletes all your data** — no backups, no cloud storage

## AI Processing

- When you generate a packing list, your trip description is sent to **Google's Gemini API**
- Google processes your description to generate list suggestions
- **Google's privacy policy applies** to this processing: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **We do not retain copies** of your descriptions — they are only used for the immediate API call

## Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:
- **Internet** — Required for AI generation via Google Gemini API
- **Storage** — Required for sharing packing lists via the native share sheet

## Children's Privacy

PackRight is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

## Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, please contact us at: **feedback@packright.app**

*This privacy policy is intentionally simple and transparent. We believe in minimal data collection and maximum user control.*
