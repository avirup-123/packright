Your Packing Checklist for Europe 👇

Europe spans over 40 countries, dozens of climates, and thousands of years of culture — which makes packing for it genuinely tricky. A summer trip to the Amalfi Coast calls for completely different gear than a winter break in Prague or a backpacking run through Scandinavia. Add to that the mix of budget airlines with strict carry-on rules, cobblestone streets that destroy the wrong shoes, and a culture of dressing up slightly more than you might at home — and the generic packing list you find on most travel blogs is almost never right for your specific trip.
