Expert Travel Tip: Pack a traditional Tenugui or hand towel

Many public restrooms in Japan do not provide paper towels or hand dryers, and active travelers will find them essential for wiping hands. Additionally, if you plan to visit a traditional *onsen* (hot spring) or *sento* (public bath), carrying a small quick-dry towel or *tenugui* is required for washing and drying off.
